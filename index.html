01 / THE METHOD

Symmetry sets the stage.
Autoregression fills it in.

CrystalAR generates a symmetry-constrained template, a lattice, and coordinates in sequence. Continuous geometry becomes a search through increasingly precise regions.

01

A structured template

Elements and Wyckoff positions describe the symmetry-inequivalent sites before geometry is generated.

02

Precision, level by level

Free Wyckoff parameters are refined from coarse to fine. Sites interact within each resolution level.

03

Symmetry by construction

Symmetry operations expand representative sites into the complete crystal, preserving their constraints.

Symmetric, stable, unique & novel

The default model discovers 161 novel unique templates. The flexible-size variant explores 268, with a lower S.S.U.N. rate of 0.19%.

S.S.U.N. adds a non-P1 requirement to S.U.N.; it does not by itself mean the Wyckoff template is novel. Template novelty ignores element identity.
